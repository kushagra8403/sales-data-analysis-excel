# Key findings

This case study evaluates campaign efficiency using spend, leads, conversions, revenue, ROAS and CAC.

- Channel-level analysis shows how acquisition efficiency varies across marketing channels.
- ROAS is paired with CAC so revenue efficiency is not viewed in isolation.
- Campaign-level results identify outliers for deeper investigation rather than relying only on channel averages.

**Business takeaway:** compare efficiency and scale together, then validate campaign quality and attribution before reallocating budget.
