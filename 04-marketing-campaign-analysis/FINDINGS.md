# Findings Framework

Run the generator and analysis to reproduce channel-level spend, revenue, leads, conversions, ROAS and CAC.

## Questions for a BI review

- Which channels combine strong ROAS with meaningful conversion volume?
- Which channels have high CAC?
- Are the highest-spending campaigns also producing efficient returns?
- Where is additional budget most defensible based on both efficiency and scale?

## Business actions to test

- Separate efficiency metrics from volume when reallocating budget.
- Investigate low-ROAS campaigns before simply cutting the whole channel.
- Compare CAC and conversion quality, not clicks alone.
- Use controlled budget tests before making permanent allocation changes.

## Limitation

Attribution is simplified and the dataset is synthetic.
