# Executive Findings Framework

Run the generator and analysis to reproduce the 24-month executive KPI model.

## Questions for leadership

- How are revenue, profit and margin trending?
- Is customer and order growth supporting revenue performance?
- Is marketing spend translating into efficient revenue generation?
- Are service-level issues moving alongside commercial performance?

## Dashboard principles

- Show current KPI, prior-period comparison and trend together.
- Separate commercial, customer, marketing and operational signals.
- Use drill-downs for explanation rather than overcrowding the executive page.
- Label synthetic data clearly.

## Limitation

The model is synthetic and intentionally simplified. It demonstrates dashboard structure and metric relationships, not a real company's performance.
