# Key findings

This case study brings commercial and operational KPIs into one executive reporting model.

- Revenue, profit, margin, orders and AOV provide the commercial view.
- Active customers, marketing spend and ROAS add customer and acquisition context.
- SLA breach rate adds an operational service-quality measure to the executive view.

**Business takeaway:** an executive dashboard should connect financial performance with customer, acquisition and operational indicators so changes in one area can be investigated alongside the others.
