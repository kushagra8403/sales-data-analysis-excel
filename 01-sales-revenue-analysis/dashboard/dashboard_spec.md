# BI Dashboard Specification

## Executive Overview
KPI cards: Total Sales, Total Profit, Profit Margin %, Orders, Average Order Value, Customers.

Visuals:
1. Monthly Sales & Profit trend
2. Sales by Category
3. Profit by Region
4. Sales by Channel
5. Top 10 Products by Sales

Filters: Date, Region, Category, Channel, Product.

## Product & Margin Analysis
- Product sales ranking
- Product profit ranking
- Margin % by product
- Discount band vs margin
- Units sold by product

## Regional Performance
- Sales by region
- Profit by region
- Margin % by region
- Orders by region
- Monthly trend for selected region

## Design principle
Every visual should answer a business question. Revenue should not be shown without profit/margin context.
