# Findings & Recommendations

## Headline results
- Total sales: £2,277,461.02
- Total profit: £397,729.15
- Overall margin: 17.46%
- Orders: 3,000
- Customers: 448
- Average order value: £759.15

## Findings
### 1. Category concentration
Technology is the largest revenue category at £1,462,351.71. This indicates a material dependency on technology sales.

### 2. Regional performance
London leads sales at £517,202.74. Regional decisions should use profit margin as well as revenue.

### 3. Channel mix
Online produces the most revenue at £1,128,336.06. Channel profitability should be reviewed before increasing investment.

### 4. Discounting
Compare revenue and margin across discount bands. Promotions should be judged on incremental profit rather than sales uplift alone.

## Recommendations
1. Set category and regional targets using both sales and margin.
2. Review low-margin products before increasing promotional spend.
3. Use discount bands to identify a sustainable promotion range.
4. Investigate the strongest regions and channels for repeatable growth.
5. Refresh the dashboard monthly and use exception reporting.

## Limitation
The dataset is synthetic. Findings demonstrate analytical methodology rather than claims about a real retailer.
