# Findings

The synthetic dataset contains 3,000 orders across UK regions, categories and channels.

## Headline KPIs

- Sales: approximately £1.88m
- Profit: approximately £322.6k
- Overall profit margin: approximately 17.2%
- Orders: 3,000
- Customers: 450
- Average order value: approximately £625.75

## Business questions

- Which categories and products contribute most revenue and profit?
- Which regions produce the strongest margin?
- How do online, store and corporate channels compare?
- How does sales performance change over time?

## Recommendations to investigate

- Prioritise high-margin products rather than revenue alone.
- Compare channel economics before increasing promotional activity.
- Investigate regional differences in both sales and profit.
- Monitor discounting because additional revenue is not automatically additional profit.

## Limitation

This is a synthetic portfolio dataset created for demonstration and reproducibility.
