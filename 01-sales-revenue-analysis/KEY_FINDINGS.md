# Key findings

This case study analyses retail revenue and profitability across products, regions, channels and time.

- Revenue and profit are tracked together so growth can be assessed alongside margin.
- Category, regional and channel breakdowns identify where commercial performance is concentrated.
- Monthly analysis highlights changes in demand and seasonality for further investigation.

**Business takeaway:** sales growth should be evaluated alongside profitability, product mix and regional/channel performance rather than revenue alone.
