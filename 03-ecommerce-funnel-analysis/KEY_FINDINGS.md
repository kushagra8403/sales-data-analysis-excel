# Key findings

This case study measures the customer journey from product view through purchase across traffic sources and devices.

- The funnel quantifies where sessions are lost between product view, cart, checkout and purchase.
- Conversion is compared across acquisition sources to identify meaningful differences in traffic quality.
- Device-level analysis helps separate acquisition performance from potential mobile or desktop experience issues.

**Business takeaway:** prioritise the largest measurable funnel drop-offs and validate them against real UX, campaign and checkout data before changing spend or product flows.
