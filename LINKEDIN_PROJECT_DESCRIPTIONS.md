# LinkedIn project descriptions

## 1. Sales & Revenue Analytics
End-to-end retail analytics project using Python, SQL and Excel. Analysed revenue, profit, margin, products, regions and sales channels, then translated the results into a Power BI dashboard specification. Synthetic data is reproducible and clearly labelled.

## 2. Customer Churn & Retention
Customer analytics project using Python and SQL to measure churn, active MRR and revenue at risk. Segmented customers by plan and tenure and designed retention-focused KPI views for further investigation.

## 3. E-commerce Funnel Analysis
Analysed 12,000 synthetic sessions across product view, cart, checkout and purchase stages. Compared conversion and revenue by traffic source and device using pandas and SQL, then defined a BI funnel dashboard.

## 4. Marketing Campaign Performance
Analysed campaign spend, leads, conversions, revenue, ROAS and CAC across marketing channels. Built a reporting approach that considers efficiency and scale together rather than relying on a single metric.

## 5. Operations & Workforce Analytics
Built an operational workforce scorecard covering hours, absence, output, SLA breaches and quality across teams and shifts. Used SQL and pandas to structure the analysis for operational BI reporting.

## 6. Executive BI Dashboard
Created a 24-month executive KPI model covering revenue, profit, margin, active customers, orders, AOV, ROAS and SLA breach rate. Designed an executive dashboard structure linking commercial, customer, marketing and operational performance.
