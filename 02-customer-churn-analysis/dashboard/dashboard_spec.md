# Power BI dashboard specification

Pages: Executive Retention Overview; Segment Risk; Customer Drivers.

Cards: churn rate, active customers, active MRR, revenue at risk, average tenure.

Visuals: churn by plan, tenure, acquisition channel; revenue-at-risk bars; scatter of satisfaction vs usage coloured by churn; slicers for plan/channel/tenure.

Recommended action view: identify high-value churned customers and low-tenure/high-risk segments for retention experiments.
