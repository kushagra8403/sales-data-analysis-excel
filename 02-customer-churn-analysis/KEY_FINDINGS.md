# Key findings

This case study measures churn and recurring-revenue exposure across a synthetic subscription customer base.

- Overall churn rate and monthly revenue at risk are calculated directly from the generated customer data.
- Churn is segmented by subscription plan and tenure so retention teams can investigate higher-risk groups.
- The analysis separates active MRR from churned-customer revenue exposure.

**Business takeaway:** use segment-level churn and revenue-at-risk together rather than looking at churn rate alone. Validate these patterns against real customer behaviour before taking retention action.
