# Findings Framework

Run the generator and analysis to reproduce team and shift performance.

## Questions for a BI review

- Which teams have the strongest balance of output and quality?
- Does higher absence coincide with lower quality or output?
- Which shifts show more SLA breaches?
- Are additional hours associated with proportional increases in output?

## Business actions to test

- Investigate teams with both high workload and weaker quality.
- Review shift-level SLA patterns before changing staffing levels.
- Track output per hour rather than output alone.
- Pair operational metrics with employee-support context before drawing causal conclusions.

## Limitation

The dataset is synthetic and does not capture real workforce policy, scheduling or case complexity.
