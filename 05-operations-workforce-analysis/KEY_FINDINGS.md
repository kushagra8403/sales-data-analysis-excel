# Key findings

This case study evaluates workforce productivity and service performance across teams and shifts.

- Output is compared with weekly hours to understand productivity rather than workload alone.
- Absence and quality are tracked alongside output to avoid treating volume as the only performance measure.
- SLA breaches are segmented by team and shift for operational investigation.

**Business takeaway:** workforce decisions should balance output, quality, attendance and service-level performance rather than optimise a single metric.
